How this is calculated — Both figures assume quarterly compounding (the standard method for cumulative/reinvestment FDs in India), applied to the savings balance too, so the comparison is like-for-like for the same money over the same tenure.

About the rates — Reference rates shown are indicative, sourced from published bank rate cards as of August 2026, for domestic deposits below ₹3 crore. Banks revise FD and savings rates periodically in line with RBI policy — always confirm current rates on the bank's own website or app before booking.

Tax note — FD interest is taxable at your slab rate. TDS applies once interest crosses ₹40,000 a year (₹50,000 for senior citizens) per bank. This tool does not account for tax, penalties for premature withdrawal, or minimum-deposit rules — it's for estimation only, not financial advice.
